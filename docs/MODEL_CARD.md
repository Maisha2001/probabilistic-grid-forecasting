# Model card

**Task:** hourly demand prediction at 1, 6, 24 and 168 hours for five archived
Australian regional series and their algebraic aggregate. Intended use is
reproducible forecasting research and a local demonstration.

**Data:** pinned, licensed `tsibbledata` source files for NSW, QLD, SA, TAS and
VIC, using `OperationalLessIndustrial`. The baseline uses 2012–2014; the forward
experiment extends through February 2015. Half-hour observations are averaged
to hourly MW. A physical UTC alignment is unverified. `ALL_FIVE` is a constructed
sum, not an independently measured national target. See [DATA.md](DATA.md).

**Models:** seasonal persistence with residual quantiles; horizon-specific
quantile XGBoost; a 57,762-parameter temporal patch transformer trained from
scratch. Transformer seeds 42, 43 and 44 are all reported. Inputs are past demand
and known calendar cycles. Weather and future demand are excluded.

**Protocol:** rolling chronological folds, training-only scaling, separate
selection and calibration blocks, and fixed models within each test period.
Tree candidates and transformer epochs are chosen before calibration. The
January–February 2015 forward experiment retained settings chosen for the 2014
comparison. Both evaluations have now been inspected; neither is an untouched
test for future model tuning. Detailed protocols and all predictions are linked
from the README.

**Uncertainty:** shrinkage-covariance reconciliation followed by split
calibration for simultaneous regional bands. Nonnegative leaf points and band
endpoints sum to the aggregate. Temporal dependence violates the assumptions
needed for a simple exchangeability-based coverage guarantee. Aggregate bounds
are envelopes, not marginal quantiles.

| Evaluation | Calibrated tree MASE / joint coverage | Calibrated transformer MASE / joint coverage |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 2014 matched-cutoff comparison | 0.771 / 73.6% | 1.090 / 71.1% |
| 2015 forward test | 0.879 / 72.6% | 1.202 / 88.7% |

**Failure evidence:** trees are more accurate, while their joint bands
under-cover. The forward transformer's wider bands do not demonstrate reliable
calibration across dates or failures. A 24-hour synthetic observation outage
reduces matched daily-origin joint coverage to 37.5% for trees and 74.4% for the
transformer. Peak slices and regional distribution shifts expose additional
variation. Missing values are repaired causally; repair does not restore coverage.

**Serving:** version-checked CPU inference, four horizons and six nodes per
request, archived replay or 337 hours of custom leaf history. Trained weights
remain local and are regenerated by the pinned pipeline. The interface supports
data inspection and model comparison, not automated grid control. No external
credentials, pretrained models or paid inference are required.

**Limits:** old and geographically narrow data; incomplete national-demand
coverage; unverified physical clock alignment; small and dependent calibration
samples; wide aggregate envelopes; synthetic rather than observed sensor failures;
no live weather or exogenous demand model; and device-dependent training results.
Do not use these forecasts for operational dispatch or assume accuracy transfers
to another meter, region or period without a new evaluation.
